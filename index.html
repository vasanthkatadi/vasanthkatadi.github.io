Method

Design principles

What makes an evaluation useful?

01

Real dependency structure

Multiple subsystems interact, so solving one local symptom cannot accidentally solve the task.

02

Independent verification

The grader checks observable behaviour and invariants rather than trusting the candidate's implementation path.

03

Adversarial edge cases

Tests target superficially plausible repairs: stale state, leakage, aliasing, shape coincidence and order dependence.

04

Failure analysis

Successful and failed trajectories are evidence. I look for shortcuts, unintended solution paths and verifier blind spots.

About

Katadi Suvarna Vasanth Raidu

I work on evaluation problems for advanced coding and reasoning agents. My focus is realistic software-engineering environments, scientific computing, robust verification, and understanding why capable models fail on long-horizon tasks.

I enjoy problems where correctness is not visible from one output: state has to survive replay, numerical methods have to stay stable, compilers have to preserve meaning, and evaluation pipelines have to avoid leakage.
